# Task 17 – Customer Segmentation using RFM Analysis

## Project Overview

This project focuses on Customer Segmentation using RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) Analysis.

RFM analysis is a data-driven technique used to understand customer purchasing behaviour and divide customers into meaningful segments based on their transaction history.

## Objectives

- Analyze customer purchasing behaviour
- Calculate Recency, Frequency, and Monetary values
- Assign RFM scores to customers
- Segment customers based on their RFM scores
- Identify valuable and loyal customers
- Generate useful business insights

## RFM Analysis

### Recency
Measures how recently a customer made a purchase.

### Frequency
Measures how frequently a customer made purchases.

### Monetary
Measures how much money a customer spent.

## Customer Segmentation

Customers were grouped into different segments based on their RFM scores. These segments help businesses understand customer value and create appropriate marketing strategies.

## Key Insights

The analysis helps identify:

- High-value customers
- Loyal and frequent customers
- Recent customers
- Customers who may need re-engagement
- Customers with lower purchasing activity

These insights can help businesses improve customer retention, targeted marketing, and customer relationship management.

## Conclusion

RFM analysis provides a simple and effective way to segment customers according to their purchasing behaviour. The results can help businesses focus their marketing efforts on valuable customers while developing strategies to re-engage inactive customers.
